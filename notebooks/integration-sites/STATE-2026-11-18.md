## 2. Decisions applied today

### 2.1 The assignment floor is 6 bp, with exact matching below 15 bp

`min_assign = 6`, new constant `exact_below = 15`. **These are one decision, not two.** Under the
`L %/% 10` mismatch budget a 6-base clip is allowed one mismatch, and **21 of the 35 molecules at
6 bp then hit one of the 100 decoy flanks**; under exact matching, none do. Lowering the floor
without the companion change would have been strictly worse than leaving it at 10.

Measured per base, computed each run (`clip_floor_curve.csv`), exact-match columns:

| clip length | molecules | exactly one real site | hit ≥1 decoy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 bp | 21 | 2 | **21 of 21** |
| 4 bp | 17 | 13 | 10 of 17 |
| 5 bp | 9 | 8 | 1 of 9 |
| **6 bp** | **35** | **27** | **0** |
| 7 bp + | 748 | all | 0 |

**Not purely additive.** Against the old rule the change gains **57** three-prime assignments
(6/7/8/9 bp = 25/20/4/8) and **loses 5** (10/11/12 bp = 1/1/3) that had rested on a mismatch at a
length where the decoys say a mismatch is unsafe.

The mono-replicate control, recomputed after the change, agrees in the band that matters: the 96
molecules at 6–9 bp contribute **zero** cross-mouse errors, and the pooled rate improved from 0.62%
to **0.50%** (7 → 5 landings) because two of the five rejected assignments were themselves errors.

`min_assign` has now been 8, 10 and 6. 8 came from the wild-type tubes being empty, which bounds
cross-tube hopping rather than within-tube chance matching — the wrong instrument. 10 came from the
right instrument read at the wrong resolution: decoy hits in coarse bands, with a mismatch allowed.

### 2.2 Cell evidence is restricted to Cell Ranger's called barcodes

33 of what the cell table called cells **were not cells**: none appears in any
`sample_filtered_feature_bc_matrix/barcodes.tsv.gz`, so none is in the Seurat objects either (18 in
Rx_4, 12 in Rx_6, one each in Rx_2/Rx_3/Rx_5). The BAM carries error-corrected `CB` tags for droplets
Cell Ranger did not call and the pipeline read them without checking, so the ambient population was
arriving as cells — 2.8% of the table. 34 removed on the current run.

The filter is applied **where cells are counted and deliberately not to the clip and genomic
tables**, because `ambient_rate()` is computed from exactly the rows it removes. The barcode list is
captured with the run in `work/called_barcodes.csv.gz` so downstream counts need no access to `data/`.

### 2.3 Every reported number is computed, not typed

Five measurements existed only as prose in the notebook, while twelve chunks recomputed around them —
they would have gone stale silently at the first upstream change. Now pipeline outputs read by render
chunks: `clip_floor_curve.csv`, `repertoire_completeness.csv`, `unassigned_molecules.csv`,
`ambient_rate.csv`, and `work/barcode_counts.csv` for the droplet denominators.

The mono-replicate control needs the hashtag calls from the targets store, so it stays a notebook
chunk. It also had to **move** in the document: it was written into the floor section where
`site_replicate` and `embedding` are a thousand lines from being defined, and would have failed at
render.

### 2.4 One matcher, several views

`clip_site_matches()` is the only code that decides whether a clip matches a flank. It records the
**mismatch count** per clip × flank × orientation rather than a 0/1 verdict, so a view can ask a
different question — exact matching is `nm == 0`, a different floor is one argument — and the gate
can prove the views agree. `assign_clips_exact()` is a filter over it, and the floor-plus-tolerance
rule lives in a single `assign_keep()` predicate used by both the assignment view and the gate.
With two copies, changing the floor would have left the gate passing against the old rule.

### 2.5 Writes verify themselves

The `/well/ludwig` fileset hit its 391 TB limit with ~23 GB free and writes into `output/` began
returning **short files with no header, no error and no warning** — a deliberate 5,000-row write
produced 88 lines while the same write to `/tmp` was complete. Four outputs were corrupted that way,
one of them `cell_site_assignments.csv`, which the report reads. `write_csv_checked()` counts the
lines back and errors on a mismatch; all 17 writes use it, and it then caught three further
truncations and halted. The two large artefacts are gzipped (18× smaller). **Neither guard should be
removed** — the fileset is shared and the condition will recur.

## 4. Claims withdrawn or corrected today

1. **The `z = 6` channel-overlap excess is withdrawn.** It was an artefact of counting the same
   molecule in two channels. With `m` on distinct molecules the totals read 83 against 109.8
   predicted, where they had read 150 against 109.8 — the prediction sits between the two counts, so
   the overlap is consistent with the per-molecule model.
2. **λ from the channel overlap is invalid** and is not computed. It inherits the inflated `m` and
   ran 1.17×–2.22× above the molecule-count λ at every site where both were defined.
3. **"177 spurious *Ddx10* UMIs"** was wrong — IS02 is antisense to *Ddx10*, so none of those reads
   is counted.
4. **The leakage attribution is withdrawn in both its versions** (not B0301, not B0304). It was a
   composition, not a rate: B0304 has 92.0% of its cells in the mCherry⁻ fraction. Per cell the
   animal effect is 2.4-fold (0.054% / 0.103% / 0.130%), and against each animal's own mCherry⁺ rate
   B0304 leaks the **least** (1.6% vs B0303 2.7%, B0301 5.8%).
5. **"Selection, not leakage" for the 100%-mCherry⁻ sites was wrong.** Discovery pools every tube's
   clips and assignment tests every flank against all of them, so the asymmetry is measured, not
   constructed. Thin at 2–6 cells, but real.
6. **`f` = 1.8% was circular** and is replaced by the decomposition in §5.
7. **Two counts in the last render were wrong and are fixed**: the wild-type tubes hold **one**
   called cell carrying a junction, not two — the Rx_5 index-hopping barcode was never a called cell
   and the filter removes it — and the genomic decoy scatters over **7** positions, not 9.

The gate caught two real bugs on its first run, which is the argument for making it an equality that
halts: a union of matches across a molecule's reads let a 5-base read's matches through a floor

dplyr evaluates sequentially. The gate also belongs on **reads**, not molecules — the assignment tier
is a per-read filter, so only a per-read view reproduces it to the row.
